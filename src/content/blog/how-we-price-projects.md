## We're Not Cheap

And we're not sorry about it.

A lot of agencies will underbid to win projects. They'll promise the world, deliver mediocrity, and disappear when things go wrong. We don't do that.

### Why We're Not Cheap

**Good people cost money** - We hire the best engineers, designers, and strategists. They're expensive. And worth it.

**Quality takes time** - We don't cut corners. We don't ship code we're not proud of.

**We stick around** - We maintain your product, fix bugs, and help you scale. That costs money.

**We're profitable** - We don't need to underbid to survive. We can afford to be selective.

### How We Price

We don't have a fixed menu. Every project is different. Here's what we do:

1. **Understand your problem** - We spend time learning what you actually need
2. **Estimate the work** - We break it down into tasks and estimate honestly
3. **Quote transparently** - We tell you what it costs and why
4. **No surprises** - No hidden fees, no change orders every week

### What You're Paying For

- **Expertise** - Years of experience solving similar problems
- **Quality** - Code that's maintainable, tested, and documented
- **Communication** - Regular updates, clear explanations, no BS
- **Reliability** - We deliver on time, we respond to emails, we care about your success

## The Alternative

You can go cheaper. You can find agencies that will underbid us. But you'll get:

- Junior developers working on your project
- Code that's hard to maintain
- Missed deadlines
- Agencies that disappear after launch
- Projects that cost 2x more to fix than they did to build

We've seen it happen. We've cleaned up the mess.

## Our Bet

We believe that quality is worth paying for. We believe that long-term relationships matter. We believe that your success is our success.
