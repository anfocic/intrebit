## The Office is Dead

We don't have an office. We never will.

Our team is distributed across time zones. We work from coffee shops, home offices, co-working spaces, and wherever we're most productive. And you know what? We're more productive than we ever were in an office.

### Why Offices Don't Work

**Interruptions** - Someone's always stopping by your desk. "Got a minute?" No, I don't. I'm in flow.

**Commute** - 1-2 hours a day wasted sitting in traffic. That's 250 hours a year. That's 10 days of work.

**Forced socializing** - Not everyone wants to chat in the break room. Some people work better alone.

**Meetings** - So many meetings. Meetings about meetings.

### Why Remote Works

**Focus** - Deep work happens when you're not interrupted.

**Flexibility** - Work when you're most productive, not when the office is open.

**Talent** - We can hire the best people anywhere, not just people who live in our city.

**Life** - You get your time back. More time with family, hobbies, rest.

## The Trade-offs

Remote work isn't perfect. You need:

- **Async communication** - Not everyone's online at the same time
- **Clear documentation** - You can't just ask someone at their desk
- **Trust** - You have to trust your team to get work done
- **Intentional connection** - You have to work harder to build relationships

But the benefits far outweigh the costs.

## The Future

Remote work is here to stay. Companies that force people back to the office will lose talent. Companies that embrace remote work will win.

We're betting on remote. And we're winning.
